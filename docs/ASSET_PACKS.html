What to buy around KayKit

KayKit Complete stays the game's base. This page weighs three ways to buy out the rest, and checks each against 172 needs, from the first frame through every age to Space.

The answer

Buy first

Kenney All-in-1

$19.95 once · CC0 · every future pack included
  • UI skins for every window: UI Pack, RPG Expansion, Fantasy UI Borders, and UI Pack – Sci-Fi for the later ages.
  • 180 cursors, input prompts and fonts.
  • Particle, smoke and light-mask textures for the effects.
  • About 1,200 sounds and music loops: interface, impacts, RPG, sci-fi, jingles, voice.
  • Train, Factory and Industrial kits for Steam → Automated; the Space, Station and Modular Space kits as parts for the Port and base; the Modular Cave Kit for the mines.
Then

Quaternius

Standard parts free · Source via Patreon (35–545 NOK a month) or per pack, e.g. $14.99
  • Ultimate Monsters and the Bestiary for the creatures in the mine lairs.
  • Animated robots, mechs and aliens for the Robots milestone and Space.
  • Ultimate Space Kit and Ultimate Spaceships; Sci-Fi Essentials' animated screens; the Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit for a habitat interior.
  • Modular Train and Steampunk Turret for the Steam age.

Synty

$0 now · $99.99–$199.98 if it ever happens
  • Its INTERFACE HUD packs are the richest ready-made HUD art there is: Fantasy Warrior HUD has the WoW-style frames, bars and minimaps.
  • But the prefabs and animations only work in Unity and Unreal; the art clashes with KayKit's characters; and neither Synty licence says anything about browser games.
  • Ask Synty in writing first. Then, if at all, buy Fantasy Warrior HUD outright.
  • Never SyntyPass: once it lapses you may not use the assets in new work (§6.2), and a game still being developed needs an active subscription (§12.3). That means paying for ever.

How far that gets us. Even with everything bought, about 6 in 10 needs still take some of our work: laying out the HUD in HTML and CSS (packs only supply the art), recolouring and kit-bashing models, and composing scenes. About 6% can't be bought at all.

The minigame boards are the clearest case. Whatever we buy, only 1 of 21 boards is covered off the shelf. Packs give the parts: gauges, bars, frames, particles, 3D props. The boards themselves are ours.

What decides most of it is the browser. A browser game sends its model, image and sound files to every player, where anyone can save them. CC0 (KayKit, Kenney), CC BY with credits, the Quaternius licence were considered in this historical research. Sonniss recordings have since been withdrawn pending source redistribution review. The Unity Asset Store and Layer Lab forbid letting others extract their assets, and Synty doesn't say.

Free, right away: Google Fonts for three or four era faces; game-icons.net for ability, Road, status and map glyphs (credits required); the Sonniss GDC archive for ambiences, machines, steam, electricity and launches; CC0 music loops from Abstraction; three.quarks for authoring particle emitters.

What stays ours, whatever we buy

By rough estimate, 60–70% of the remaining art and UI effort sits in the middle four groups.

  • BrandThe logo, wordmark and title card.
  • HUD and window compositionEvery widget's layout, behaviour and animation; packs only skin them. Above all the Road of Invention window, Field Notes, plan cards and the tower panel.
  • All 21 minigame boardsHardest: the live circuit board with its water view, the transformer and zone-refining boards, the Morse tape, radar scope, relay ladder and punched cards.
  • The rocket and the flightCore stage, boosters, upper stage and fairing, the descent and ascent stages, the return capsule with heat shield and parachute, the Port's pad and tower. KayKit has no rocket and no antenna.
  • The tower's own partsGem windows, 14 gem variants in three grades with their effects, element emblems, and devices sized to the windows.
  • World systems kept in codeTerrain, roads, river, the day and night sky, fog of war, light pools, the stone bridges, the endless mines, Space's ground and sky. They beat any pack here.
  • Era props no pack hasTelegraph key and sounder, relay boxes, battery cells, transformer, bellows, gold pan, sieve and still, lathe, welding torch, punched-card machine, governor, bells.
  • RecoloursKayKit Space Base's warm swatches, any red Quaternius planets or rocks, Kenney's saturation.
  • Audio directionPicking and editing from Sonniss and Kenney; a soundtrack per age (no pack has one); voice barks.
  • A crew suit on the KayKit rigIf KayKit's own "space ranger" doesn't suit.

The three stacks

Each stack is what we'd add around KayKit Complete and the free sources. They are the three ways to buy out a whole library: one cheap CC0 generalist, the stylised-3D specialist closest to KayKit with a painted UI kit, and the big commercial ecosystem with era-themed HUD kits. On itch.io's 3D best-seller list, KayKit holds 14 of the first 36 places, Quaternius 10, and the Kenney All-in-1 is third.

A · $19.95 · CC0

Kenney All-in-1

What's in it

More than 60,000 assets, 1,200+ sounds and loops, UI, icons, cursors and fonts, in PNG, SVG, OBJ, FBX, glTF and OGG. New packs are free for owners; 2026 added a Modular Space Kit, a Modular Cave Kit and Light Masks.

Why

It fills every category KayKit lacks (UI, cursors, particles, sound, fonts) under a licence the browser can't break.

Weak spots

A flat, saturated, toy-like look; generic cartoon UI; a pixel-art minimap pack; no skill icons; no real-world rocket confirmed.

B · free tier + Patreon · QAL; Layer Lab from $15.99

Quaternius + Layer Lab

What's in it

84 Quaternius packs: monsters, robots, mechs, aliens, space and sci-fi kits, trains, nature, and an animation library that retargets to Mixamo-style rigs. Layer Lab sells painted UI as plain PNG kits (GUI Pro – Fantasy RPG, $39.99: 1,200+ PNGs, 46 screens).

Why

The stylised 3D closest to KayKit for the later ages, Space and animated creatures; the best-looking painted UI that ships as files.

Weak spots

No effects or sound; Quaternius humans use another rig and adult proportions; Layer Lab's licence bans any form that lets others extract the files.

C · per pack $9.99–$499.99, or SyntyPass

Synty

What's in it

POLYGON packs for every era (Fantasy Kingdom, Western, Town, Military, Office, Sci-Fi Worlds with a rocket, Sci-Fi Space with planets and asteroids), INTERFACE HUD kits, a 520-icon pack, a Particle FX pack, animation packs. No audio.

Why

The only vendor with complete era-themed HUD kits, and a catalogue that spans medieval to sci-fi.

Weak spots

Unity and Unreal prefabs; FBX instead of glTF; realistic proportions that clash with KayKit's chibi cast; roughly $1,000–1,500 for what we'd need; nothing on browser use.

Mixing the stacks

  • Scale. KayKit is the reference (a person is 0.5 world units). Kenney props, machines and space parts fit after one scale factor per kit; Quaternius props, ships and creatures fit, but its humans look tall and thin next to KayKit; Synty props read as over-detailed, fine at a distance or in their own scene.
  • Colour. KayKit uses one gradient atlas per pack, so it stays crisp at any size and recolours by repainting swatches. What pulls everything together already exists: every material becomes Lambert and gets the shared world patch (painted finish, rim light, colour grade, fog). Add a grade per kit and a swatch remap that keeps every warm red out of Space.
  • Rigs. Heroes, NPCs and humanoid enemies stay KayKit (23-joint rig, one clip library). Other packs add creatures that carry their own clips, played as they are. Other packs' humans appear only in isolated scenes, if at all.
  • Formats. KayKit, Kenney and Quaternius ship glTF and fit the asset build as it is; Synty needs Blender (centimetres to metres, axes, materials rebuilt).
  • The import pipeline normalises provenance (SHA-256 pins, licence files, generated credits), scale and axes, materials, palette, geometry merging, names, animation maps, icons, 9-slice UI, audio loudness and loop points, and particle atlas slots. Only what's used ships.

The walk-through

What a player meets, area by area, and how each part is made today. The codes in bold point to rows in the matrix.

First load, the title and the HUD
  • First frame. There is no loading screen and no title screen. Terrain, water, grass and sky are generated and on screen at once; models stream in (src/main.ts). Asset need: none, but there is no logo or wordmark anywhere (U01; the favicon is an inline SVG shield).
  • First start. The Class window opens with three cards: Knight (Tank), Warchief (Support) and Mage (Magic). Each has a portrait rendered from its 3D model, a blurb and its skills (U02, I05; C + R).
  • LAN.
    • A join card: your name and the address others use.
    • A slim bar: players, ping, Pause / Save / Load with a save code.
    • A "New version ready · Update (U)" banner and a paused overlay. (U22; C)
  • The HUD (src/ui/hud.ts, hud.css; all C, gold-on-dark gradients, system Palatino serif):
    • Top bar: gold coin, lives heart, wave number and timer, Call wave, a 1× speed toggle, the age badge (opens the Road), ☰, and the DEV badge with its clock (U04, U26).
    • Player frame: portrait, level, HP and XP bars, name. Also a target frame and a boss frame (U05, U06).
    • Tracker: wave intel and objectives (U11).
    • Action bar: skills 1–4 (inline-SVG ability icons), the interact slot, the plan bar on 5 … =, and the Plan Book and Buy buttons (U07, I04).
    • Bags: the tool row and an item grid with stack counts (U08).
    • Details panel for whatever is selected (U09).
    • Messages: event log, toasts, the cursor hint and the placing banner (U12).
    • Minimap with the vale and mine maps (M makes it big) (U13; D).
    • Tooltips (U10).
    • Panels for later systems: the drone box (battery, hull, range), the Space panel, flight rows (U23, U24).
  • In the world:
    • nameplates, loot names (Alt) and site checklists (labels.ts);
    • floating damage and crit numbers (fx.ts);
    • health and progress bars, ground rings and the blueprint hologram (overlays.ts);
    • the CSS vignette and hit flash. (U14, U15, V13)
  • Windows (src/ui/windows.ts):
    • Shop, with seven stores and tool and plan-scroll cards;
    • Storage;
    • Plan Book (P) and Buy a plan (G);
    • the Road of Invention with its Field Notes (O);
    • Menu (Esc) and Class (C). (U02, U03, U18, U19, U20)
  • Cursors are the OS cursor (U16). Fonts are the system serif (U17).
  • Sound: synthesised UI tick, open and deny (A01).
The vale
  • Ground and water:
    • terrain with a painted splat texture drawn on a canvas (W01, P);
    • roads (W02, P);
    • the river, fords and swimming (W03, P);
    • mighty stone bridges, one deck between solid parapets (W04, P);
    • the mountain ring (W05, P);
    • haze and fog of war (P).
  • Sky and light:
    • a shader sky dome with sun, clouds, moon and stars, and day and night on an 8-minute day (W06, P);

  • Scenery:
    • Trees are generated in code (treegen.ts). KayKit Forest Nature is imported but not loaded yet (W07, W08).

    • Crypts, graveyards and fences are KayKit Halloween (W12, K).
    • Camps, the Lumber Camp, chests, ruins and the shrine (W13, K).
    • Scrap heaps (W14, P), quarry rocks and crystal clusters (W15, P + K), mine heads and cave mouths (P).
  • Light sources and power:
    • braziers (W16, P);
    • painted light pools and blob shadows (V12, P);
    • power poles with wires and electric lamps (W17, P);
    • the watermill / hydro generator (W18, K, no broken state).
  • People and siege: footmen, captains, porters and townsfolk (W19, K). Catapults, carts and ships (W20, K).
  • Building: sites with the blueprint hologram, scaffolds and stacked parts (W21). Damaged buildings smoke, become ruins and get repaired (W22).
  • As the ages advance: street lamps, wires, carts becoming trains, signs (W23, —).
The hero
  • Classes (content.ts CLASSES):
    • Knight: 600 HP, sword and shield.
    • Warchief: 460 HP, fights with the tools he buys, and has the Scout Drone.
    • Mage: 320 HP, staff and bolts. Today these are the KayKit 1.0 Knight, Barbarian and Mage on the old 41-bone rig. The 23-bone Rig_Medium cast is imported and waiting (C01–C03).
  • Later classes: Druid, Engineer, Ranger, Rogue, Paladin, Cleric (C04, K, imported).
  • Gear:
    • weapon tiers sword A–G, axe/hammer/staff A–D, bow A–C, shields A–D (C05);
    • tools in hand: axe, pickaxe, hammer, shovel (C07);
    • visible armour tiers don't exist (C06).
  • Animations:
    • locomotion: walk, run, strafe, back-pedal, jump;
    • wading and swimming, climbing a tower;
